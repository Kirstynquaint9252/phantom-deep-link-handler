This application bridge allows your computer to handle request signals from mobile Ethereum applications. It directs traffic between your web browser and the Phantom mobile wallet. This process enables you to sign transactions and connect your wallet through a link.

## 🛠 Features

*   **Wallet Integration:** Syncs your mobile wallet with desktop browsing sessions.
*   **Transaction Processing:** Handles signing requests for blockchain assets.
*   **Connection Management:** Maintains a secure link between apps and your device.
*   **Universal Support:** Works with standard web3 protocols on Ethereum.

## 🚀 Setting Up the Connection

The application runs in the background. You do not need to open a specific window to use it. When you click a connection link on a website, the software wakes up to manage the flow.

1. Open your web browser.
2. Navigate to the website that needs your Phantom wallet.
3. Click the "Connect Wallet" button on that webpage.
4. A small prompt from the application will appear on your screen.
5. Follow the directions in the prompt to confirm the link between your browser and your mobile device.
6. Once linked, you can confirm transactions on your mobile phone when the desktop app sends a request.

## 🔧 Troubleshooting Common Issues

### The application does not open
Check if the installer finished correctly. Restart your computer and try to launch the shortcut from your Start menu again.

### Requests do not reach the mobile device
Ensure your phone and computer currently use the same network or have a stable cellular connection. Verify that the Phantom app on your phone is open and logged into the correct account.

### Connection errors
If a website reports a connection error, refresh your browser tab. If the problem persists, close the link handler from your system tray (the small arrow near your clock) and reopen it from the Start menu.

### Clearing cache
If the tool behaves in an unusual way, you can reset its temporary data. Right-click the icon in your system tray and select "Reset Application". This clears old sync data without removing the software.

## 📝 Frequently Asked Questions

**Does this software store my wallet information?**
No. It only translates communication signals between your browser and your wallet. No private information stays on your disk.

**Do I need to keep this running?**
Yes. The software must be active as a background process to catch incoming signals from your browser. 

**Does this work with other wallets?**
This tool focuses on the Phantom wallet standard. It might not work with other wallet providers.

**Is this safe for corporate devices?**
The software is lightweight and runs with standard user permissions. It does not tamper with system files outside of its own directory. Always check with your IT department if you have strict security policies.

**How do I remove the software?**
Open your Windows "Settings" menu. Select "Apps" and then "Installed Apps". Locate "phantom-deep-link-handler" in the list. Click the three dots next to the name and select "Uninstall". Follow the removal prompts to delete the application files from your drive.
